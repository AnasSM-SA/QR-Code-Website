# QR Studio
# https://anassm-sa.github.io/QR-Code-Website/

QR Studio creates QR codes in your browser. No account or server is needed, and your QR content is not saved.

- QR codes for URLs, text, email, phone numbers, and WhatsApp.
- English and Arabic interface with light, dark, and system themes.
- Foreground and background colors with a low-contrast warning.
- Live preview and PNG download.
